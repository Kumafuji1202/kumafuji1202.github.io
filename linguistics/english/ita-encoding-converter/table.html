  • white background signifies that the character was present in pre-18.0 Unicode and the scheme maps the characters according to Unicode.
  • gray background signifies that the codepoint is mapped to a different character in Unicode.
  • red background signifies that the character is problematically unified in Unicode 18.0 with a character already present in pre-18.0 Unicode.
  • green background signifies that the character is added to Unicode in Unicode 18.0 and the scheme encodes the character as in Unicode.
  • cyan background signifies that the character is mapped to a codepoint in the Private Use Area in the Basic Multilingual Plane of Unicode.

Pitman ITA Capitals are only supported by PitmanITA’s encoding. Other letters are encoded the same in all of the encoding schemes.

As for the letterforms, as of now (2026 Nov 7th), PitmanITA, Phonemics, Xim Sans and Catrinity support all correct letterforms. Other fonts used here have incomplete support of correct letterforms.
