* Phonemics’ encoding doesn’t have a codepoint for the “half a”. ** I couldn’t find a two-storey bottom-connected a-u ligature in Nishiki-teki.

Pitman ITA Capitals are only supported by PitmanITA’s encoding. Other letters are encoded the same in all of the encoding schemes.

As for the letterforms, as of now (2026 Nov 7th), PitmanITA, Phonemics, Xim Sans and Catrinity support all correct letterforms. Other fonts used here have incomplete support of correct letterforms.
